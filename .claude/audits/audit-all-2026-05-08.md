## Executive summary

**164 findings across 14 audits — 3 blocker / 28 major / 61 minor / 72 suggestion** (1.8% blocker rate; rubric §4 respected, no skill exceeds 40% blocker share). Biggest skill clusters: `audit-security` (21), `audit-state-and-recovery` (20), `audit-code-smells` (18). Ship-blockers are concrete and narrow:

1. `sec:eb479d86:ssh-pin-bypass` — `setup/upload.go::uploadISOsViaSCP` hardcodes `StrictHostKeyChecking=accept-new` and ignores the `knownHostsPath` returned by `sshpin.Verify`, defeating the Proxmox host-key pin on the very phase that uploads ignition ISOs (which carry the cluster pull-secret). High confidence, low fix-risk.
2. `sec:06f00bcb:auth-tree-in-webroot` — `setup/apache.go::DeployToWebServer` copies `auth/kubeconfig` and `auth/kubeadmin-password` into apache's DocumentRoot relying solely on file-mode bits. One `chmod -R go+r`, one openshift-install mode-default change, or one DocumentRoot override exposes them via HTTP. Fix: `<Location /auth> Require all denied`.
3. `tst:bfdaf5e3:cred-bytes-type-untested` — `internal/config/secret.go::SecretBytes` (the wrapper for `ProxmoxConfig.Password`/`APIToken`) has zero direct tests; `String()=='[redacted]'` and Zeroize-overwrites-prior-Set invariants are unguarded. A future refactor that drops `Redacted()` or returns `Bytes()` from `String()` silently leaks credentials through every fmt-formatted log call site.

The single highest-leverage **theme** spans 4 audits: **credential lifecycle at the executor seam.** Sibling exec wrappers diverge — `terraform.Executor.ZeroizeEnv` and `okd.Provisioner.ZeroizeEnv` exist; the canonical `executor.Executor` does not. Cross-references: `sec:7b2829bb:executor-no-zeroize` (major) ↔ `api:7b2829bb:zeroize-asymmetry` (major) ↔ `err:7b2829bb:exit-error-no-redact` (major; ExitError carries Stderr without `Redacted()`) ↔ `obs:5013fea6:stderr-attr-leaks-subprocess-output` (major; sink-side mirror) ↔ `sec:35abd54e:env-string-residue` (major; `Env() []string` immutable copy survives Zeroize). One coherent fix: add `ZeroizeEnv` + `Redacted()` to the canonical executor; promote the duplicate bodies; teach `RedactHandler.secretKeyFragments` to redact `stderr`.

Second theme: **typed-error mapping skips the cobra boundary.** `err:fd2125dd:cli-bare-errors-skip-typed-mapping` (major) ↔ `ux:e7db1220:releases-validate-flag-error-not-usageerror` (major) ↔ `ux:fd2125dd:args-validator-not-usageerror` (major) ↔ `err:c19ee328:phase-step-bare-fmt-errorf` (minor). CLI subcommands return `fmt.Errorf` for usage, kubeconfig, and cluster failures — `exitCodeFor` defaults all of them to 1 instead of 64/66/4. ~50 sites; the fix is a `errtypes.UsageError` wrap at the cobra boundary plus a phase-step wrap helper.

Third theme: **state durability gaps.** `state:48688e63:tf-state-no-backup` (major; no okdctl-managed snapshot before apply/destroy), `state:4c092fce:tf-state-no-lock` (major; no `-lock-timeout`), `state:62cb8a95:state-version-warn-only` (major; `checkStateMajorVersion` only on destroy), `state:c19ee328:setup-installer-already-done-only-bootstrap` (major; manifests AlreadyDone too coarse), `state:15ba17da:destroy-iso-cleanup-before-tf` (major; cleanup runs after partial destroy), `state:fb54208a:postinstall-bootstrap-cleanup-nonfatal` (major; zombie bootstrap risks etcd quorum but reports complete).

Two prior blockers from 2026-05-05 are **resolved** in-tree this run:
